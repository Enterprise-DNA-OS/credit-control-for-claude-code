# The credit desk without a front end

A credit controller checks overdue invoices, discusses exceptions with account owners, records a promised date and writes a review. Those jobs can run from a database and short commands. Read-only reports show the result.

A screen offers quick browsing, mobile interaction and an inbox next to the account. This free version does not provide those, offline mobile sync, customer payment pages or automatic email delivery. It also does not have Chaser's live accounting connections. The database and draft workflow are useful from reviewed exports alone. Enterprise DNA can build the screens and connections you need as part of your installed version.

brand.json controls the statements, dispute briefs, promise records and reports. /new-view adds a view without creating a new app. /customise changes the records and rules through migrations. The database stays yours.
